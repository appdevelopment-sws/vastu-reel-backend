Child Safety Policy

Child Safety Standards & CSAE Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

We are committed to maintaining a safe and respectful environment for all users of our real-estate video-sharing platform.

Our application is designed specifically for sharing real-estate-related content, including property videos, plots, houses, apartments, interiors, exteriors, construction projects, and other property-related media.

1

Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

We have a zero-tolerance policy for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

Our platform strictly prohibits any content that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children. Users may not upload, share, distribute, request, promote, or otherwise make available any content involving the sexual exploitation or abuse of minors.

Any such content identified on our platform will be subject to immediate action, which may include removal of the content, suspension or termination of the responsible account, and reporting to appropriate authorities where required by applicable law.

2

Permitted Content

Our platform is intended exclusively for real-estate-related content, such as:

  • ✓ Residential and commercial properties
  • ✓ Land and plots
  • ✓ Houses, apartments, villas, and buildings
  • ✓ Property interiors and exteriors
  • ✓ Construction and development projects
  • ✓ Property tours and walkthroughs
  • ✓ Real-estate listings and promotional videos
  • ✓ Architecture and property-related information

Content unrelated to real estate may be restricted or removed in accordance with our platform rules.

3

Prohibited Child-Safety Content

The following content is strictly prohibited:

  • ✕ Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • ✕ Sexualized images or videos involving minors
  • ✕ Sexual exploitation or grooming of children
  • ✕ Content that promotes or facilitates the sexual exploitation of children
  • ✕ Requests for, distribution of, or links to CSAM
  • ✕ Any other content that sexually exploits or endangers minors
4

Reporting Violations

Users can report content or accounts that they believe violate our Child Safety Standards or involve the exploitation or abuse of children.

Reports should include sufficient information to help us identify the content or account involved.
